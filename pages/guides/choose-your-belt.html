Who each belt is for.

Yellow Belt

For team members who support improvement work without leading it: operators, administrators, analysts and anyone who sits close to a process that is being fixed.

You learn the shared language (waste, variation, DMAIC) and how to contribute data and ideas.

Green Belt

For people who lead improvement projects next to their day job: team leads, engineers, quality and operations staff.

You learn to run a project through DMAIC, collect and read data, find root causes and test a fix.

Black Belt

For people who lead larger, cross-functional projects and coach others, often full time on improvement.

You add deeper statistics, designed experiments, change leadership and the coaching of Green Belts.

What a project looks like.

  1. Yellow: a contribution

    You help a team with a defined task: mapping a step, timing a process, gathering a week of data or testing an idea. You are not expected to own the project.

  2. Green: one real problem, start to finish

    You pick a problem in your own area, write a charter, measure the current state, find root causes, test an improvement and hand over a control plan. Many programmes ask for one completed project.

  3. Black: bigger and across teams

    The problem spans departments and has real money or customers attached. You lead people who do not report to you, and you coach the Green Belts working alongside.

That is about 19,000 defects per million opportunities, a process yield of 98.10 % and a sigma level of about 3.6 (with the conventional 1.5 shift).

Sigma level uses the conventional 1.5 sigma shift: sigma = z(1 − DPMO ÷ 1,000,000) + 1.5. Without any defects there is no sigma level to calculate.
